# Medical Equipment Sales Analysis

## Context
A medical equipment supplier in Ikeja wanted to understand which products sold consistently and which were at risk of remaining in stock or expiring.

## Analysis
I analyzed sales of equipment models and disposable products, customer preferences for new and fairly used products, and competitors’ strengths. I also used sales trends to support product planning.

## Outcome
The analysis helped inform purchasing decisions and reduce orders for products that were unlikely to sell and could be damaged during storage.

## Tools
Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, Power BI, Tableau
