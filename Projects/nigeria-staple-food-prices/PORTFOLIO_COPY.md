## Nigeria Staple Food Price Trends Across Markets

Built an end-to-end analysis of World Bank modeled food-price estimates across 67 Nigerian local markets from 2007 to 2026. Used Python and pandas to validate, clean, reshape, and aggregate market-month estimates for beans, gari, maize, rice, sorghum, and yam; prepared MySQL schema and analytical queries; and created Power BI-ready market, commodity, national-trend, and state-trend datasets.

**Tools:** Python, pandas, MySQL, Power BI

**Key observation:** In the August 2026 snapshot, modeled year-over-year estimates increased for beans (1.8%) and yam (1.1%) and decreased for gari, maize, rice, and sorghum (12.4%–19.9%). The comparison uses equal-weighted market means and is descriptive; it does not explain causes or imply household affordability.

**Portfolio bullets:**

- Built a reproducible pandas workflow to validate source quality and reshape market-level close estimates into an analysis-ready fact table.
- Developed national and state trend outputs, including month-over-month and year-over-year changes, for Power BI and MySQL analysis.
- Documented source-unit differences, excluded regional/national summary rows to avoid double counting, and recorded the market-count discrepancy for transparent interpretation.
